# Finance integrity repair — 30 September 2026

This release keeps the existing app, encrypted storage, bank connection and separate partner profile.

## Corrected

- Bank snapshots no longer rewrite opening balances. Missing history remains an explicit discrepancy; backfilled history does not change a newer bank snapshot.
- Failed multi-record saves roll back. Import cursors advance only after a successful import.
- PocketSmith validates amounts, dates, account mapping and AUD currency. Malformed or truncated upstream history fails visibly. Daily complete snapshots flag source rows that disappear.
- Matching requires a unique merchant match; two external transactions cannot consume one legacy transaction. Source categories, review flags and user category overrides are retained.
- CSV imports are additive, reviewed and account scoped. Repeated purchases are retained and balances retain their own dates. Ambiguous single-day statement balances are not inferred.
- Finance record collections merge individual records and retain alternatives. Deletions have versioned tombstones. Multi-key incoming saves have recovery journals.
- Spending and savings suggestions pause for stale, unreconciled or unresolved essential accounts. Partner suggestions inherit this gate.
- Funding includes direct salary; the tracked wealth position includes overdrafts. Allocation checks share available cash across goals, funds, bill reservations and buffers.
- Month navigation avoids timezone rollover errors. Budget changes have dated snapshots; historical comparisons are labelled against the current plan.
- Imported display text and links are escaped/validated in repaired views. Dialog fields have associated labels; keyboard activation and contrast are improved.
- Overview starts with data confidence and two actions, with further detail collapsed. Tax-file wording describes encrypted sync accurately and new attachments have a size limit.

## Verification and remaining boundaries

215 automated tests pass, including rollback, cursor, malformed upstream, deduplication, date, overdraft, sync merge and privacy regressions. The 11 main screens were exercised at 390 px without horizontal overflow or console errors using isolated synthetic data. Manual balance entry was checked through the UI: a $50 test discrepancy stayed visible.

Both private pages were rebuilt with the existing encryption keys and verified by decryption. No production transaction records or private Gist contents were altered for tests.

This is a defensive repair release, not certification of historical bank data. Older forced opening balances cannot be reconstructed without source statements. Live reconciliation, source-deletion review and outstanding token setup must complete before the financial recommendations can be relied on. Notion has no configured server connection; the app explicitly links to the existing Money Hub and does not claim automatic Notion sync. Principal-only debt estimates remain labelled estimates. GitHub Gist does not provide a transactional multi-device database; record merge reduces lost edits, but simultaneous remote writes still require careful review. Storage quota is handled visibly, not removed.
